I spent about ~3 days trying to get the simpletest working and was finally able to do it.
Note, this lab is hard to do incrementally. The MVP to get simpletest passing took quite some
thinking and debugging.
The issue I was frequently running into was this, lets say we have Parent process P and child process C.
C is created from forking P. While C exited it was freeing P's pages. This happened in a chain, wherein
the cowtest process would exit and free memory, but it would free pages need by sh, which would then
free pages needed by the init process. The scause register had 0xc and 2 as values in the exception
which made me suspect that I was somehow freeing the code segment which would be read-only.
I was in the woods trying a lot of options but none of them worked. 
There were multiple things that could go wrong like maybe my ref counting approach was wrong etc.
I then got the ingenious idea (after a literal walk in the park), why don't I print the pagetable
of the parent before forking and the child after forking and track the ref counts of all the physical
pages. The refcounts of all the physical pages must be 1 greater than the parent's physical pages
before forking. And et voila, that was indeed the bug. While forking the process, I wasn't maintaining
the refcount of the Read-only pages. On forking, the refcount for a Read only page will be incremented
by 1. I fixed that and then simpletest passed. This was a simple 2-line change which took me 3 days to debug.

When I ran usertests -q, some tests were failing but this was more of an exercise in handling
all edge cases. Now, all usertests pass.
